# WiproTalentNextJavaFSD

## An Approach to Logic Building

### Prereq1: Conditional Statements
- **prog1.java**: Write a program to accept a number N and print whether the number is EVEN or ODD.
- **prog2.java**: Write a program to accept two numbers and print whether their sum is EVEN or ODD.

### Prereq2: Looping Constructs
- **prog1.java**: Write a program to print all numbers from 1 to 100 i.e. 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 . . . 98 99 100
- **prog2.java**: Write a program to print alternate numbers starting from 1 to 99 i.e. 1 3 5 7 9 11 13 . . . 95 97 99
- **prog3.java**: Write a program to print alternate numbers starting from 0 to 100 i.e. 0 2 4 6 8 10 12 . . . 96 98 100
- **prog4.java**: Write a program to print all numbers backwards from 100 to 0 i.e. 100 99 98 97 96 . . . 4 3 2 1 0
- **prog5.java**: Write a program to print numbers backwards from 100 to 1 by skipping 2 numbers i.e. 100 97 94 91 88 85 82 79 . . . 22 19 16 13 10 7 4 1

### Prereq3: Pattern Printing
- **Problem1.java**: Print the below shape on a console window [10 rows right-angled left justified numbers]
- **Problem2.java**: Print the below shape on a console window [10 rows right-angled right-justified stars]
- **Problem3.java**: Print the below shape on a console window [10 rows triangle of incr-decr numbers]
- **Practice1.java**: Print the below shape on a console window [10 rows triangle incr-nums decr-alphabet]
- **Practice2.java**: Print the below shape on a console window [10 rows rhombus inc-decr numbers]
